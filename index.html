About Our School

St. Kalemba S.S Villa Maria sits among green hills with red-roofed classroom blocks, dormitories, laboratories and playing fields. Founded on Catholic values, the school offers a balanced education that combines strong academics, music, sports and spiritual growth.

Our crest carries a graduation cap, pens and an open book, symbols of learning, under the German motto Der Herr ist mein Hirt.
